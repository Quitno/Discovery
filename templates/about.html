About us

Find the right place, with clearer information.

PataMahali is a Kenya-focused property discovery platform for rentals, homes for sale, land, hotels and short stays, shops, offices and warehouse space. Search from county down to constituency, ward and neighbourhood.

Trust matters.

Published listings must pass the platform's account, payment and property-review checks. A verification badge is not a substitute for visiting the property, confirming authority to let or sell it, checking documents, and using safe payment practices.

Operated by Toror Technology & Innovation Ltd. PataMahali is a property discovery platform; the landlord or authorised agent remains responsible for the property and the accuracy of its advert.
