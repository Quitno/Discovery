Toror Technology & Innovations Ltd

Practical technology. Useful digital products.

Toror Technology & Innovations Ltd (also referred to as Toror Technology and Innovations Ltd or Toror TRT) is a Kenyan technology company focused on building and operating digital products, web platforms and business software. Its work brings together practical problem-solving, accessible design and technology that supports everyday business and customer needs.

What the company works on

Custom web applications, business portals, retail and inventory workflows, point-of-sale tools, product-data and barcode solutions, and online platforms.

Software development and digital solutions in Kenya

Toror Technology & Innovations Ltd works across custom software development, web application development, business automation, POS software, inventory management, barcode and product-data tools, and online marketplace development.

The company's technology focus includes:

  • Custom business software, dashboards and web applications.
  • Point-of-sale, inventory and barcode-based retail workflows.
  • Online marketplaces, directories and customer-facing platforms.
  • Software integrations and digital tools designed around real operational needs.

Working in Kenya

Toror Technology & Innovations Ltd builds for practical use in the Kenyan market, with an emphasis on clear interfaces, mobile access and systems that can support the way local businesses and customers work.
